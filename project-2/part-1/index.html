    Mademoiselle Caroline Rivière

    by Jean-Auguste-Dominigue Ingress

    The portrait of Mademoiselle Caroline Rivière was painted in 1806 by the French Neoclassical artist Jcan Auguste Dominique Ingress, and today hangs in the Louvre Museum. It is the third of three portraits of the Rivière family the artist painted that year.

    Date: 1806
    Medium: Oil on Canvas
    Dimensions: 100 x 70 cm
    Home: Musèe du Louvre, Paris
